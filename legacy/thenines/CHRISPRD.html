The Bubble Challenge
Christmas Version

35 Bubbles

It has 15 large bubbles and 20 small ones. The bubble shapes vary in size from 3 to 9. There are 87 unique shapes in the puzzle.

It is important to know the shapes. Review them before you start:

  • Shapes 1–24
  • Shapes 25–48
  • Shapes 49–72
  • Shapes 73–87

The object of the challenge

Initially, 10 of the bubbles are filled in. They are RED and cannot be changed.

Fill the remaining bubbles with the numbers 0 to 9. You score points for each shape that contains no duplicate numbers. The larger the shape, the higher the points.

Score high enough to unlock the art and poetry.
