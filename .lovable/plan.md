
# APPs2400 — Modern Board Game Company Website

A bold, playful redesign of the APPs2400 Canadian Board Game Company website with modern design, social media integration, e-commerce UI, and customer service features.

---

## 1. Global Layout & Navigation
- **Sticky top navigation bar** with the APPs2400 logo, nav links matching the original site's tabs: Home, Board Games, Online Games Individuals, Reserve A Game, Online Tournaments, Small Business Partnerships, Charity Fund Raising Partnerships, Buy Games, Registration, Login/Logout
- **Mobile hamburger menu** for responsive design
- **Footer** with social media icons (Facebook, Instagram, X/Twitter, YouTube, TikTok), quick links, newsletter signup, and copyright

## 2. Home Page
- **Hero section** with a bold animated banner featuring the mission statement and a CTA button ("Enter Tournaments")
- **Tabbed content carousel** replicating the original's rotating sections: Mission Statement, Vision Statement, Tournaments, Pokerstr8ts Fundraisers, Pokerstr8ts Donors — redesigned as modern cards with playful colors and icons
- **Download section** promoting the free Pokerstr8ts standalone game with a prominent download CTA
- **Featured games grid** showcasing board game thumbnails in a visually appealing card layout

## 3. Board Games Page
- Grid of board game product cards with images, titles, and short descriptions
- Playful hover animations and category filters

## 4. Online Games / Tournaments Pages
- Information about online game tournaments
- Tournament entry CTA linking to centriko.com
- Scoring and event code information displayed clearly

## 5. Buy Games (E-commerce Storefront UI)
- Product listing page with game cards showing images, prices, and "Add to Cart" buttons
- Product detail view with game description, images, and variants
- Shopping cart slide-out panel with item list, quantities, and total
- Checkout page UI (mock — no real payment processing)

## 6. Partnerships Pages
- **Small Business Partnerships** — info section with benefits, how it works, and a contact/inquiry form
- **Charity Fund Raising Partnerships** — similar layout with charity-focused messaging

## 7. Registration & Login Pages
- Modern registration form with fields for name, email, password
- Login page with email/password fields
- Both are UI-only (no backend auth)

## 8. Reserve A Game Page
- Reservation form with game selection, date picker, and contact info

## 9. Customer Service
- **Contact page** with a styled contact form (name, email, subject, message)
- **FAQ page** with an accordion of common questions about tournaments, games, partnerships

## 10. Social Media Integration
- Social media links in the footer and a dedicated "Follow Us" section on the home page
- Social media share buttons on product/game pages
- Embedded social feed placeholder section on the home page

## Design Direction
- **Bold & playful** — vibrant blues, greens, and warm accent colors inspired by board game aesthetics
- Rounded corners, card-based layouts, subtle shadows and hover animations
- Fun iconography from Lucide icons (dice, trophy, users, etc.)
- Large, readable typography with a modern sans-serif font
- Fully responsive across desktop, tablet, and mobile
